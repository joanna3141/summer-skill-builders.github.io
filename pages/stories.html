The Jungle

By Caitlyn

In a land far far away there was a jungle that flourished everyday. The sounds of squawking birds and large lion roars filled the atmosphere. Vibrant fruits were filled to the brim with juice for all to eat. At night noises began to soften and all things were not even talking, not even a peep.
